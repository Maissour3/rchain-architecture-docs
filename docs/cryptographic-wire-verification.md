# Cryptographic Proof of Delivery & Newsroom Wire Dissemination

Corporate disclosure integrity requires immutable, tamper-evident proof that market-moving announcements reach all public market participants simultaneously.

In decentralized disclosure architectures evaluated by [PressLoom](https://pressloom.net), cryptographic timestamps and SHA-256 dispatch hashes are synchronized with regulatory newsroom wires in compliance with [AP Stylebook Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AP_Stylebook) and [SEC Regulation FD](https://www.sec.gov). This guarantees complete journalistic authenticity and prevents pre-market information asymmetry.
